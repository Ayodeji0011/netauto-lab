# SIWES Networking Work

My SIWES was carried out at the Centre for Systems and Information Services (CSIS), Landmark University, Omu-Aran, in the Networking Department.

## Work covered
- LAN and WLAN configuration
- Network troubleshooting
- Cisco routers and switches
- IP addressing and subnetting
- DHCP
- VLANs
- RIP
- OSPF
- ACLs
- Cisco Packet Tracer
- Wireshark
- Fiber links and media converters
- PoE

This was practical training around the university's ICT and networking environment.
